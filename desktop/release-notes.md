**Windows** (x64): `MelinaOffice-Windows-x64.zip` holds `melinaoffice-setup.exe`, which installs Melina Office for you, without administrator rights: desktop and Start menu shortcuts, Open with and Default apps for .docx, .xlsx and .pptx, and Uninstall in Apps & features. It needs the Microsoft Edge WebView2 runtime, which Windows 10 and 11 include.

**macOS** (13 Ventura or later, Apple silicon and Intel): open `MelinaOffice-macOS.dmg` and drag Melina Office to Applications. It is signed and notarized by Apple; .docx, .xlsx and .pptx files list it under Open With.

A release may hold only one of the two while the other is being built.
